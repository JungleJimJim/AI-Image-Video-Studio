# Version 1.10.4

The Video tab restores its saved starting image after the app reopens. Choosing a Library image or importing an image into Video also remembers the selection immediately, without starting a generation or changing generation settings.

Older selected images are resolved from the full Library when they are outside the recent-image snapshot. A delayed restoration cannot overwrite an image selected in the meantime. Removed or unavailable images show a message asking for another selection.
